Privacy Policy

Effective date: [Effective Date]

This policy explains how this website and the Aqualine game handle information, in simple terms.

1. Information stored on your device

Aqualine saves your progress (levels completed, best moves, weekly points and badges) and your settings in your browser’s local storage on your device. We do not receive it unless you create an optional account: then your email address and a copy of your progress are also stored online by Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) so you can restore it on another device. You can log out or delete your account in the game’s Settings, which deletes the online copy. Clearing your browser data removes the copy on your device.

2. Technical information

When you visit, our hosting provider and your browser exchange technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This is needed to deliver the site and keep it secure.

3. Advertising

This site may show ads provided by Google (for example Google AdSense and AdSense for Games). Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies, and may process information such as your IP address, browser and device details and how you interact with ads. This is used to show, measure and limit ads, to prevent fraud and, where allowed, to personalise ads. We do not control how Google processes this data. Google explains how it uses data from sites that use its services in its own privacy information.

4. Rewarded and video ads

If rewarded ads are offered, you only see one if you choose to watch it, for example to get a free hint. The same advertising information described above may be processed.

5. Third-party services

Ads may be provided by Google and its partners. They have their own privacy policies.

6. How information is used

Local game data is used only to run the game and remember your progress. Advertising information is used by the advertising services to show and measure ads.

7. Sharing with service providers

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with our hosting provider, with Google and its partners as part of showing ads, as described above, and with Google Firebase if you create an account.

8. Children’s privacy

Aqualine is a general puzzle game and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please contact us.

9. Data security

Progress data stays on your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your choices

You can clear your browser data to delete saved progress, manage or block cookies in your browser, and manage ad personalisation in your Google account’s ad settings. Where required in your region, you may be shown a choice about cookies and ads.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will change when we do.
