HOW TO PLAY

One glowing tank is the water source. Tap any pipe to rotate it a quarter turn.

Water spreads through every connected branch, splitting at T and cross junctions.

Fill every empty ball to finish. Reaching only some of them does not complete the level.

Fewer moves earn a better score. Hints highlight one pipe that needs turning.
